Hangboard Timer privacy policy

By PierDo · Effective September 11, 2026

This policy covers Hangboard Timer for Android, package com.pierdo.hangboardtimer.

Your routines stay on your device

Your routine names, intervals, preferences and session history are stored locally. Hangboard Timer does not upload these records, create an account, provide cloud sync, use advertising SDKs, or track how you use your routines. The app does not access your camera, microphone, device location, contacts or body measurements. Timer records describe elapsed intervals and your timer controls, not measured exercise.

Optional purchases

The free timer needs no purchase. If you open purchase options, Google Play and RevenueCat process purchases and verify access to the one-time Pro upgrade. They may receive a purchase identifier, an app-specific anonymous customer identifier, purchase history and transaction status, approximate country and technical information such as app version, platform and IP address. Payment details are handled by Google Play; Hangboard Timer does not receive your card number.

Optional tips are separate repeatable purchases. They do not unlock Pro or add any other digital benefit. The app saves a small local purchase-status record to avoid repeating an uncertain payment. Purchased access can be cached for offline use and checked with RevenueCat when the app is online.

See Google's privacy policy and RevenueCat's privacy policy. Purchase-related data is used for app functionality, transaction handling, purchase analytics and fraud prevention; we do not sell it or use it for advertising.

If PierDo provides a complimentary access code, redeeming it stores a local access record. The code is checked on your device and is not sent to a server or included in exported backups.

Screen-off timing and permissions

A foreground service and temporary wake lock keep a session you start running while the screen is off. A visible notification shows the session and lets you stop it. Notification permission is requested before starting a session. Sound uses media volume; vibration is optional. The service stops when the session finishes or you stop it, and sessions are limited to 55 minutes. The app does not automatically start sessions when the device boots.

Backups and deletion

You can export routines, settings and history to a JSON file you choose. The app does not upload exports. Import merges valid records without importing purchase access or resuming a session. Android automatic backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for this app.

Delete routines or clear history in the app. Uninstalling removes local app records. Files you exported remain wherever you saved them. Deleting local records does not cancel, refund or delete Google Play transaction records; Google and RevenueCat may retain purchase records as required to process purchases, provide restores and meet their obligations.

Contact and requests

For privacy questions or a request concerning purchase-related data, contact pierdo.dev@gmail.com. Please name the app and describe the request. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. We may need enough information to identify the purchase and verify the request.

Changes

Updates to this policy will show a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app and store disclosures.
