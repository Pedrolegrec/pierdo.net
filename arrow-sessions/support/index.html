Your archery scorebook

Save an equipment setup and start a session. Choose your distance, units, target maximum and arrows per end. Tap a score for each arrow; M records a miss and X counts as ten on a ten-point target.

Correct and compare

Undo the last arrow or edit a recorded end. Finish a session to include it in matching-session trends; reopen it if needed. A saved session retains its equipment snapshot even when you later edit that setup. Matching comparisons use distance, target format and equipment details; unlike sessions are excluded.

Keep your records

Export a JSON backup from Settings. Import merges new records and skips identical ones. Conflicting versions are rejected without changing your scorebook; preserve both files for review. CSV is a readable scorecard, not a restorable backup. Backups never grant Pro or reviewer access.

Permanent Pro

Pro adds multiple saved setups, reusable rounds and detailed matching-session comparisons. History and export stay free. Use Restore purchase with the Google Play account that purchased Pro. Do not start another purchase while a payment is pending.

Storage trouble

If a save fails, the prior saved record remains intact. Check free device storage and close other Arrow Sessions windows before retrying. Do not clear app data to fix a save problem.
