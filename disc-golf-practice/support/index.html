Disc Golf Practice support

Your offline putting and approach notebook, by PierDo.

Get help

Email pierdo.dev@gmail.com with the app version and a description of the problem. Include your Android version if relevant. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Continue a practice

Use Resume practice on the Practice screen. A selected number is saved locally before you confirm the batch. Undo restores the last result for editing. Skipped batches add no attempts to your statistics.

Keep a backup

In Settings, choose Export JSON backup and select a destination. Import merges matching backups, preserves existing records and rejects conflicts. CSV is for reading your results in other tools; JSON is for restoring them. Uninstalling or clearing app storage can remove your records.

Pro and restore

Pro is a permanent purchase for unlimited saved routines and matching-session comparisons. Free practice, history, charts and backups remain available. To restore, use the Google Play account that purchased Pro, connect to the internet, open Pro in Settings and choose Restore purchase. If an order is pending, check Play orders and restore before starting another checkout.

Your records and privacy

The app records results you enter; it does not measure throws or provide GPS. See the privacy policy for local storage, purchase processing and data-deletion requests.
