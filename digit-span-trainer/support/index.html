Using Digit Span Trainer

Digit Span Trainer is a digit span practice app. You see or hear a string of digits, repeat it back, and the app keeps your levels, scores and statistics on your device. It needs no account.

Digit Span Trainer for iPhone

The iPhone app has no account and no sign-in. It has no AI feature and sends no training data to a server. It offers the free app, an optional one-time Digit Span Lab unlock, and four optional tips.

Where your data lives

Your rounds, scores, streaks, settings, themes and Lab programs are stored on your iPhone only. We do not receive them. Deleting the app removes them.

Export and import

In More, then Settings, use Export CSV and Import CSV. They cover training rounds only. Export opens the iPhone share sheet and the file goes where you choose; the app does not upload it.

Restore Digit Span Lab

Digit Span Lab is a one-time unlock, not a subscription. To restore it on the same Apple Account, open More, then Settings, and choose Restore Lab purchase. You can also open More, Explore upgrades, then Purchase and restore details.

Tips

Tips are optional, repeatable purchases found at the bottom of the More tab. They add no features.

Reset all progress

More, then Settings, then Reset all progress removes your rounds and statistics from the app. Export a CSV first if you want to keep them.
