Using Disc Golf Practice

Disc Golf Practice for iPhone is an offline notebook for putting and approach practice. You set the distances and targets, throw, and record how many discs landed where you were aiming. The app does not measure throws: it keeps the results you enter.

Practice

On the Practice tab, start a putting ladder, an approach ladder or a quick single-distance practice, or build a custom practice with your own distances, target radius, discs per batch and throw style. Record each batch, including zeroes, skip a batch, undo the last result or correct it. You can pause a session and resume it later, or finish early. Play safely and check that your throwing area is clear.

History and Progress

History lists every saved session, with no limit. Progress shows results and charts for sessions that match in distance, target and throw style. In Settings you can switch between metres and feet and choose a light, dark or device-matched appearance.

Export, import and backups

In Settings, choose Export JSON backup or Export CSV history. The file opens in the iPhone share sheet so you can save it to Files, send it or print it. Import JSON backup merges a verified backup into your records without deleting existing ones; conflicting versions stop the import. Backups never include purchases.

Records are stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app can remove them, so export a backup before changing phones or deleting the app.

Pro and restoring a purchase

Practice, full history, charts and backups are free. Pro is an optional one-time purchase through the App Store, with no subscription. It adds unlimited saved custom routines and matching-session comparisons with condition filters. Open Settings, then Explore Pro, to see the price and buy. To get Pro back after reinstalling or on a new iPhone, open Settings, then Explore Pro (it reads Manage Pro / restore once Pro is active), and tap Restore purchase with the same Apple Account.

Tips

Settings also has an optional support section with four one-time tip amounts. Tips are separate, repeatable purchases and add no features.

Privacy

There is no account and no sign-in. See the iPhone privacy policy.
