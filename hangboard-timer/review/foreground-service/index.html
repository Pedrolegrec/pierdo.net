This recording shows a user starting an interval routine, the timer continuing while the app is in the background, and the ongoing Android notification used to pause the session and stop background timing.

The foreground service starts only after a user starts a session. It keeps the native timer and local phase cues working, provides a visible Stop action, and stops when the session ends. Sessions are limited to 55 minutes and do not restart on boot.
