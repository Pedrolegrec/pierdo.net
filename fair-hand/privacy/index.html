Fair Hand privacy policy

Fair Hand: Block Puzzle by PierDo · Effective October 15, 2026 · Version 1.0.0
Android package com.pierdo.fairhand

Who we are

PierDo is a sole-proprietor business in Lévis, Quebec. We make small apps. Fair Hand has no account and no server of ours.

Person in charge of personal information

The owner of PierDo is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. Contact: pierdo.dev@gmail.com, or the support page at https://pierdo.net/fair-hand/support/. We answer access and correction requests within 30 days. Never send passwords or payment-card details.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, best score, game progress, settings, sound choices and your privacy choices are stored on your phone only. We never receive them. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling removes them. You can also use Settings > Delete all data inside the app. If you share a score, the text goes only to the app you choose; we do not receive it.

Ads

Fair Hand shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on menu and score screens, short full-screen ads after the score screen, and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch. To show an ad, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK on your phone sends Google data such as your IP address (which gives an approximate location), your device’s advertising ID and app set ID, device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version), how you interacted with ads, and crash and performance information. Google uses it to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalise ads. See Google’s page Google Mobile Ads SDK: data disclosure and Google’s privacy policy.

Personalised and non-personalised ads

  • Personalised ads use your advertising ID and past activity to choose ads for you. Non-personalised ads use only the context (such as the app and a rough location) and still use identifiers for limiting how often an ad is shown and for reporting.
  • In Canada, personalised ads are off until you turn them on. Where Google’s consent form applies, such as in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google asks you on first launch.
  • Change your choice any time: Settings > Privacy > “Personalised ads” (and “Privacy choices” where Google’s form applies). You can also reset your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

Anonymous usage statistics (Google Firebase)

Fair Hand uses Google Analytics for Firebase, Remote Config and A/B Testing to count sessions, retention and purchases, and to try different ad settings on different groups of players. Firebase receives an app-instance identifier (Firebase installation ID), the advertising ID where you allowed it, device and app information, coarse location from the IP address, and the events we define. In Canada this is off until you turn on “Anonymous usage statistics” in Settings > Privacy. Google keeps this data under its own policies. Remote Config installation IDs are kept until deleted, then removed within 180 days (Firebase privacy page).

Remove ads purchase

“Remove ads” is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing, managed with RevenueCat. Google Play and RevenueCat receive purchase identifiers, an anonymous app-user ID, transaction information and device information so that your purchase works and can be restored. Your game data is not sent to them. See Google’s privacy policy and RevenueCat’s privacy policy. After you remove ads, we stop showing full-screen and banner ads. Optional rewarded videos stay available, so the ad choices above still apply.

Sent outside Quebec and outside Canada

Google (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play) and RevenueCat process data on servers outside Quebec and outside Canada, including in the United States. Google says it may process and store data anywhere Google or its agents maintain facilities. We send data outside Quebec only through the ads, statistics and purchase services described on this page.

How long

We keep nothing about you ourselves. Google and RevenueCat keep data under their own policies (see above). Data on your phone is deleted when you clear the app’s data or uninstall.

Children

Fair Hand is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you, and withdraw a consent at any time (the Settings switches do this at once). Because we receive almost nothing, requests are mostly about purchases: email pierdo.dev@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

Changes

A new effective date marks any change; material changes will also appear in the app and store disclosures.
