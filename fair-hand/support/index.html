Fair Hand help

A calm block puzzle with a fair deal, by PierDo.

How to play

  • The board is 8x8 squares. You get 3 pieces at a time.
  • Drag a piece onto the board, or tap a piece and then a square. Place all 3 pieces and you get 3 new ones.
  • A full row or a full column clears and scores points. Clear several lines at once for more points, and clear lines on several moves in a row to build a combo.
  • The game ends when none of your pieces fits on the board.
  • Fair deal: in Classic, every set of three pieces can be placed on the board it is dealt on. The Rules and Fair deal pages in the app explain how the pieces are chosen.

Daily puzzle

The Daily puzzle gives everyone the same pieces on the same day, so you can compare scores with friends. You get one attempt per day. A new puzzle is ready the next day.

Remove ads

Fair Hand is free to play with ads. Remove ads is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play. It removes the full-screen ads and the banner ads. Optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for new pieces or to continue a game, stay available. Ads never change the deal or the score.

Restore purchases

If you reinstall Fair Hand or move to a new phone, use Restore purchases in the app to bring back a Remove ads purchase you already made. Use the same Google Play account that bought it.

Privacy choices

Open Settings > Privacy options in the app to change personalised ads and anonymous usage statistics at any time. Where Google’s consent form applies, that row opens Google’s form. Settings also lets you delete all the data Fair Hand stored on your phone; a Remove ads purchase is not affected. Read the full privacy policy.
