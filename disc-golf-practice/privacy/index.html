Disc Golf Practice privacy policy

By PierDo · Effective September 19, 2026

This policy covers Disc Golf Practice by PierDo for Android, package com.pierdo.discgolfpractice.

Your practice stays on your device

Routine names, practice distances, targets, throw results, condition notes, preferences and session history are stored locally. The app does not upload those records or provide cloud sync. It has no app account, advertising SDK or behavioral analytics. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or health sensors. You measure practice distances and judge throw results yourself.

Optional Pro purchases

The free practice features do not require a purchase. Opening Pro, checking its price or restoring a purchase connects to Google Play and RevenueCat to verify the permanent upgrade. These services process purchase history and status, transaction identifiers, an app-specific anonymous customer identifier, locale and currency, and technical request information such as platform, app version and IP address. Practice records and condition notes are not sent to them. Google Play handles payment details; the app does not receive payment-card numbers.

After you use the purchase service, the app may check access again when opened or resumed online, including after refunds. It caches purchase status for offline access and retains a local pending-order guard to avoid repeating an uncertain checkout. Pro is one purchase with no subscription. Core history, export and backups remain free if access changes.

Purchase-related data supports app functionality, purchase analytics, transaction handling and fraud prevention. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. See Google’s privacy policy and RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Backups, exports and deletion

JSON backups contain routines, settings, history and any practice in progress. They do not contain Pro access. CSV exports contain recorded practice results. Files are prepared in the app’s private cache before you choose where to save or share them using Android. A destination app or service handles an export only when you choose it, under its own privacy terms. The app does not automatically upload exports.

Import validates a backup before merging records and rejects conflicting records rather than silently replacing them. Android automatic backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for this app. Keep a backup if you want to preserve your practice. Clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstalling can remove local records and cached export files. Copies you exported remain wherever you saved or shared them.

Removing local records does not refund a purchase or erase Google Play or RevenueCat transaction records. They may retain records needed for restores, refunds, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

Request deletion of purchase-related data

Email pierdo.dev@gmail.com, name Disc Golf Practice and describe your request. We may need enough purchase information to locate the relevant record and verify the request. Never send passwords or payment-card details. Records necessary for transaction, restore or legal obligations may be retained for those purposes. Local practice records are on your device and can be removed as described above.

Contact and changes

Send privacy questions to pierdo.dev@gmail.com. Updates to this policy will show a new effective date, and material changes will be reflected in the app and store disclosures.
