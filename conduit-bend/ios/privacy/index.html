Privacy policy: Conduit Bend for iPhone

Conduit Bend by PierDo · Effective October 2026 · Version 1.1.0

Conduit Bend for iPhone has no account and no sign-in, no ads and no analytics service of ours. It works offline, and we run no server for it. We do not receive your bend sheets, measurements or anything else you enter.

Your bend sheets

Bend sheets, settings and your last calculator entries are stored on this iPhone only, in the app’s own storage. Your theme choice and small counters that space out upgrade offers and the rating request are stored there too. The app does not upload any of it or provide cloud sync.

Optional purchase

Conduit Bend Pro is an optional, one-time purchase made through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the app receives no payment-card numbers. The app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service, to show the Pro price, check that a purchase is valid and restore it. Nothing is sent until you open Conduit Bend Pro in the More tab, use Restore purchase, or an upgrade offer opens and asks for the price. Your bend sheets and measurements are never sent.

The App Store and RevenueCat process purchase history and status, transaction identifiers, an anonymous customer identifier that RevenueCat creates for this install, locale and currency, and technical request information such as a device identifier, platform, app version and IP address. The price request names the screen that showed the offer, so a sale can be credited to it. That data supports app functionality, purchase analytics, transaction handling and fraud prevention. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. See Apple’s privacy policy and RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Pro is a permanent purchase, not a subscription. The app keeps your purchase status on the iPhone so Pro works offline. Use Restore purchase, in More under Purchases or on the Conduit Bend Pro screen, to get Pro back after reinstalling or on a new iPhone. Purchases are not part of backup files. The App Store and RevenueCat may keep purchase, fraud, tax or audit records under their own policies and legal obligations.

Backups and files you choose

A backup file (JSON) or a text export of a bend sheet opens in the iPhone share sheet, and you choose where it goes, for example Files. While that happens the app makes a temporary copy in its private cache and removes it afterwards. A backup holds your settings and saved bend sheets, including the sheet you are editing, and never your purchase status. Keep backups private if your sheets carry job names or other personal notes. The place you choose handles an exported file under its own terms. The app does not upload exports.

Deletion

Deleting the app removes the bend sheets on this iPhone. It does not remove files you exported, so save a backup first if you may need your sheets. Deleting the app does not refund purchases or erase App Store or RevenueCat transaction records.

Rating prompt

Conduit Bend may occasionally ask for a rating with Apple’s own rating dialog. Apple decides whether it appears. The app cannot see whether you rated or what you wrote.

More from PierDo

The More from PierDo row only opens pierdo.net in your browser when you tap it. The app sends nothing with it.

Tracking and children

The app does not display ads and does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Conduit Bend is a work tool for electricians and apprentices; we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

A calculation aid

Conduit Bend is a calculation aid. Verify every mark against local code and your bender manufacturer’s marks, and try it on a scrap piece before you bend a stick.

Purchase-related data requests, contact and changes

Email pierdo.dev@gmail.com, name Conduit Bend and describe your request or privacy question. We may need enough purchase information to find the record and verify the request. Never send payment-card details. Policy changes will show a new effective date, and material changes will be reflected in the app and store disclosures.
