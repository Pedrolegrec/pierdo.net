Using Conduit Bend on iPhone

Conduit Bend is a calculation aid. Verify every mark against local code and your bender manufacturer’s marks, and try it on a scrap piece before you bend a stick.

Marks and shrink

Pick a bend, choose the pipe, and enter the values on the fraction keypad, in inches or centimeters. Every mark is measured from the end of the pipe. Shrink is how much shorter the finished pipe ends up; add it to the length you cut. The result names the model it used: the published field multipliers, which are rounded the way tables in bender guides are, or exact geometry. It also shows the formula, so you can check it against your bender’s own guide. Change the model under Multipliers in the More tab.

A stub-up uses the take-up of the chosen pipe. You can replace it with the take-up printed on your own bender.

When a bend cannot be made

If the numbers cannot work, for example a stub-up shorter than the take-up, or bends closer together than the app allows, the app shows the cause and the nearest valid value instead of a negative or impossible mark. Change a value and try again. The closest the app allows is its own lower bound, so check your bender’s minimum distance between bends.

Bend sheet

Choose Add to bend sheet on the Bend tab, then open the Sheet tab to lay out a whole stick, 10 feet unless you change the stick length. The first bend’s distance is from the end of the pipe, and each later bend’s distance is from the last mark of the bend before it. A stub-up or kick can only be the first bend on a sheet, and the app works out its mark for you. Both saddles take the measured distance to the center of the obstruction, and the app adds the shrink to find the center mark.

Every mark is measured from the end of the pipe, and shrink adds up through the bends before it. Pipe used is the position of the last mark, and the sheet checks that it fits on the stick. The sheet you are editing is kept on the iPhone when you close the app.

Back up, share and restore a backup

Open the More tab, find Backup and export and choose Save a backup file. The iPhone share sheet opens, and you choose where to save the file, for example Files. A backup holds your settings and every saved bend sheet. To bring a backup back, choose Restore from a backup file in the same section and pick the file. After you confirm, the backup replaces the sheets and settings on this iPhone, so make a new backup first if you want to keep what is there. A backup is a single JSON file from Conduit Bend; other files are refused. Purchases are never saved in or restored from a backup. To share or print one sheet, choose Share as text file on the Sheet tab.

Included from the start, and Pro

Included from the start: the offset, the 90 degree stub-up and the 3 point saddle, the bender list (pick a hand bender by type and size, with its take-up and radius, or type in your own take-up and radius), pipe sizes up to 1 inch EMT, one saved bend sheet that you can replace with another, backup and restore, and text export. Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store, not a subscription. It adds the 4 point saddle, kick, rolling offset and parallel offset, the segmented 90 and back-to-back 90, larger pipe sizes with IMC and rigid take-up presets, and unlimited saved bend sheets. When you choose one of these, or save a second bend sheet, the app shows what Pro adds, and what you entered stays as it is until you choose. You can also open the More tab, then Conduit Bend Pro, at any time.

Restore a purchase

If you bought Pro before, on this iPhone or on another iPhone, open the More tab and choose Restore purchase in the Purchases section. You can also open Conduit Bend Pro and choose Restore purchase there. Keep the iPhone online while it checks. If checkout was interrupted, choose Restore purchase or Check purchase status before you try to buy again.

Storage trouble

If a save fails, the sheet you were editing stays on screen so you can try again. Check the free storage on the iPhone. Deleting the app removes your sheets, so save a backup file first if you can.
