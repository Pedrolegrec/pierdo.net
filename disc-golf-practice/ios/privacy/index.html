Privacy Policy — Disc Golf Practice for iPhone

Effective October 2026.

Disc Golf Practice is a manual practice notebook made under the PierDo name by the independent developer shown as the seller in its App Store listing. The iPhone app has no account and no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics service. Your practice works locally, without a server.

Local data

Routine names, practice distances, targets, throw results, condition notes, preferences and session history are stored on your iPhone only. We do not receive them. You measure distances and judge throws yourself; the app does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data.

Purchases

Purchases are optional and are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service, to check and restore what you bought. Apple processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

When you view, buy or restore Pro, or buy a tip, the RevenueCat SDK and the App Store process purchase history and transaction information (including Apple transaction identifiers), device type and operating system version, and locale and currency, as needed for purchase functionality, fraud prevention and RevenueCat's service analytics. RevenueCat creates an anonymous, install-scoped App User ID on your iPhone; it receives no name, email address or practice records from the app.

Pro is a one-time unlock, not a subscription. Optional tips are repeatable and add no features. If you delete the app or its data, you can restore Pro on the same Apple Account with Restore purchase. Apple and RevenueCat may keep purchase, fraud, tax or audit records under their own policies and legal obligations.

Export, import and backups

JSON backups and CSV exports are prepared as temporary files and opened in the iPhone share sheet; you choose where they go, and the app does not upload them. The place you choose handles an export under its own privacy terms. JSON backups contain routines, settings and history, never Pro access. Import checks a backup before merging it with your records.

Deletion

Deleting the app removes your practice records from your iPhone, so keep an export if you want to preserve them. Export files you saved or shared stay where you put them. To ask about purchase-related data, see the contact line below.

Network use and tracking

The iPhone app uses the network only for optional purchases, through the App Store and RevenueCat. It does not display ads and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites. After a practice, the app may ask for a rating through the standard iPhone prompt, which Apple runs; the app learns nothing from your answer.

Age audience

Disc Golf Practice is intended for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. Material changes will update the effective date above. Privacy questions: pierdo.dev@gmail.com.
