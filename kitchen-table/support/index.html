Kitchen Table help

A Klondike solitaire card game, by PierDo.

How to play

  • Kitchen Table is Klondike solitaire. The goal is to move every card to the four foundation piles.
  • On the table, build columns down in alternating colors. For example, a red 6 goes on a black 7.
  • Only a King, or a run of cards that starts with a King, can go to an empty column.
  • A face-down card turns face up when you uncover it.
  • Each foundation pile is one suit and builds up from the Ace.
  • Tap the stock to turn over cards onto the waste pile: one card at a time in Draw 1, three at a time in Draw 3. When the stock is empty, tapping it puts the whole waste pile back as the stock. You can do this as many times as you like.
  • Undo takes back your last move, as many times as you like.

Remove ads

Kitchen Table is free to play with ads. Remove ads is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play. It removes the full-screen ads and the banner ads. Optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch, stay available. Ads never change the deal or the score.

Restore purchases

If you reinstall Kitchen Table or move to a new phone, use Restore purchases in the app to bring back a Remove ads purchase you already made. Use the same Google Play account that bought it.

Privacy choices

Open Settings > Privacy options in the app to change personalized ads and anonymous usage statistics at any time. Where Google’s consent form applies, that row opens Google’s form. Settings also has Delete all data, which removes your games, statistics and settings from your phone; a Remove ads purchase is kept. Read the full privacy policy.
