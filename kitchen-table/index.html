Klondike solitaire with large, clear cards and winnable deals.

Kitchen Table: Solitaire is classic Klondike for your phone. Seven columns, a stock, and four foundation piles to build up by suit, from Ace to King. Play at your own pace, one deal at a time.

Winnable deals

Every deal comes from a seed number. Before a deal goes into the app, a solver plays it with every card known, face-down cards included, in the draw mode you play. The deal is kept only if the solver found a winning line from the first move. A winning line existing does not mean every way of playing finds it, and the solver knows cards you cannot see yet.

The Rules and the Winnable deals page are in Settings.

How you play

  • Draw 1 or Draw 3, your choice
  • Tap a card to play it, or drag it
  • Undo and Redo as often as you like, back to the first move
  • Hint shows one move that may help, whenever you ask
  • When every card in the columns is face up and the rest can be played out, a Finish button plays it for you

Daily deal

One deal a day, the same for everyone, in Draw 1 and Draw 3. A calendar marks the days you finished, and you can go back up to 30 days to play a day you missed. When you finish, you can share your result.

Easy to see, easy to hold

  • Large cards
  • Four-color suits
  • High contrast
  • Left-handed layout
  • Light, Dark or System theme
  • Timer on or off

Statistics

For Draw 1 and Draw 3: deals played and won, win rate, your current and longest streaks, and your fastest win.

Ads and Remove ads

Kitchen Table shows ads: a banner and full-screen ads. The Winning hint is an ad you choose to watch. After a short ad, it shows the next 5 moves of a winning line from where you are, up to 3 times per deal. Remove ads is one optional purchase, paid once. It removes the full-screen ads and the banner. The Winning hint stays, and plays a video only when you ask for it.

Your data

Your game is stored on your phone. In Settings, Delete all data removes your games, statistics and settings from this device. A Remove ads purchase is kept. The privacy policy lists the outside services the app uses.

Kitchen Table: Solitaire is available on Google Play.
