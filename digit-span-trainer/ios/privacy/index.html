Privacy Policy — Digit Span Trainer for iPhone

Effective October 2026.

Digit Span Trainer is a digit span practice app made under the PierDo name by the independent developer shown as the seller in its App Store listing. The iPhone app has no account and no sign-in, no AI feature, no advertising and no analytics service. Its training works locally without a server.

Local data

Training rounds, scores, streaks, preferences, themes, and, if you use Digit Span Lab, your saved programs, goals and the last report snapshot are stored on your iPhone only. We do not receive them. A free 7-day Lab trial, if you start it, is a local record on your iPhone; it asks for no payment details and never charges.

Digit Span Trainer uses your iPhone's text-to-speech to read digits aloud. It does not request the microphone, location, contacts or camera, and it records no voice.

Purchases

Purchases are optional and are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service, to check and restore what you bought. Apple processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

When you view, buy or restore Digit Span Lab, or buy a tip, the RevenueCat SDK and the App Store process purchase history and transaction information (including Apple transaction identifiers), device type and operating system version, and locale and currency, as needed for purchase functionality, fraud prevention and RevenueCat's service analytics. RevenueCat creates an anonymous, install-scoped App User ID on your iPhone; it receives no name, email address or training data from the app.

Digit Span Lab is a one-time unlock, not a subscription. Optional tips are repeatable and add no features. If you delete the app or its data, you can restore Digit Span Lab on the same Apple Account with Restore Lab purchase. Apple and RevenueCat may keep purchase, fraud, tax or audit records under their own policies and legal obligations.

Export

CSV export writes a temporary file and opens the iPhone share sheet; the app does not upload it. CSV covers training rounds only.

Deletion

Reset all progress in Settings removes your rounds and statistics from the app. Deleting the app removes all local data from your iPhone.

Network use and tracking

The iPhone app uses the network only for optional purchases, through the App Store and RevenueCat. It does not display ads and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Age audience

Digit Span Trainer is intended for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. Material changes will update the effective date above. Privacy questions: pierdo.dev@gmail.com.
