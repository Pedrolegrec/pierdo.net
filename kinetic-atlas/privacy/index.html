Kinetic Atlas Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-20

Kinetic Atlas is published by PierDo. This policy describes the Kinetic Atlas 0.2.1 production release candidate.

Core App And Data Stored On Your Device

The core app works without an account. Kinetic Atlas stores training state on your device, including baseline progress, current Atlas rung, completed session summaries, free camera-session usage, Coach preferences and plans, a pending cloud-selection outbox, locally cached Coach-credit balance, and local Pro entitlement state. A pending outbox can include a pseudonymous account binding so the same account can safely recover a possibly completed request without a second credit debit. The app retries the same identifier for strictly less than 30 days. At or after the 30-day device recovery horizon, it removes the pending outbox, never resends that identifier, and explains that the remote result is no longer recoverable on that device. If the Worker had already completed the paid selection, the used credit is not refunded; only a still-unresolved debit can later be reconciled or compensated. Before expiry the outbox is also removed after resolution, successful cloud-state deletion, or local app-data deletion. If a different account is chosen while an unexpired request is unresolved, Kinetic Atlas blocks the switch and preserves it until the original account recovers it or cloud state is deleted. Signing out clears the visible cached Coach-credit balance. This data is used to show your guided next session, offline Coach card, and progress feedback.

Everyone can use the deterministic, on-device Atlas Coach without a purchase, account, or internet connection. The local Coach does not call an AI service.

Kinetic Atlas disables Android cloud backup and excludes its app data from device-transfer backup. You can delete local training and Coach data by clearing app storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Camera, Tracking, And Audio

Kinetic Atlas uses your device camera for local pose detection, rep and hold counting, positioning guidance, and tracking-confidence signals during training sessions. Tracking confidence describes camera capture quality, not exercise quality or technique. Camera frames are processed on your device. Kinetic Atlas does not upload, record, or store camera video or images.

Camera access is optional for browsing progressions, using the Coach, training manually, and viewing training history. Camera-counted sessions require camera permission.

Optional spoken guidance uses on-device text to speech. Kinetic Atlas does not send or store microphone recordings or Coach audio.

Optional Cloud Coach

Anyone with a positive Atlas Coach credit balance can explicitly ask the cloud Coach to select one emphasis from a small set of app-reviewed Coach cards. This feature requires an internet connection, Google Sign-In, a RevenueCat customer linked to the same verified Google account, and a separate tap for each request. It is not free-form chat and does not run automatically after a workout.

One successful fresh selection uses one non-expiring COACH credit. Terminal responses explicitly marked debit: none and model abstentions use no credit; replaying the same completed request uses no additional credit. If RevenueCat's response becomes uncertain after debit begins, the request is not treated as a terminal failure. Kinetic Atlas reports the service as temporarily unavailable, marks the debit as resolving, and preserves the same request identifier for strictly less than 30 days. Retrying during that device window recovers or replays the paid result without a second debit. At the 30-day boundary, the app never resends the old identifier; any still-unresolved debit is reconciled or compensated during a later authenticated request or deletion from the same account. If the Worker had already completed the paid selection, abandoning device recovery at that boundary makes the result unrecoverable on that device and does not refund its used credit. Manual, offline, and local camera training remain available throughout.

After you allow cloud Coach, Kinetic Atlas immediately sends the current Google ID token and Google subject identifier to Cloudflare to verify access. The Worker derives a one-way account binding and checks the Google-linked COACH balance with RevenueCat. This access check does not include a request identifier, categorical session facts, or offered Coach-card identifiers. Google Sign-In and RevenueCat may already process the subject identifier as part of sign-in and linking the purchase account.

For a cloud selection request, the app sends to Cloudflare:

  • the Google ID token used for authentication, which can contain email, name, profile, and token-time claims;
  • a random request identifier;
  • the Google account subject identifier used for authentication;
  • a deterministic one-way account binding beginning with kacb_, derived from that subject and used to prevent account/outbox mismatches;
  • a fixed catalog version and approved candidate-card identifiers; and
  • a bounded set of categorical fact identifiers, such as whether the local Atlas repeated or advanced, whether a session used camera or manual mode, and whether camera tracking was sufficient or limited.

The app does not send camera frames, video, images, audio, pose landmarks, raw rep events, user-entered workout or Coach text, exact workout timestamps, exercise prescriptions, health or injury notes, or full workout history to the cloud Coach or its AI model.

A Cloudflare Worker validates the Google token, verifies that the same linked RevenueCat account has a positive authoritative COACH balance, enforces rate limits, and converts the Google subject identifier into a keyed pseudonymous value before storage. The Worker recomputes and compares the one-way account binding before balance or provider work. It processes the Google token only to authenticate the request and does not log or store the raw token or its profile claims.

Cloudflare's network routing transiently processes the request IP address and can derive a coarse approximate location to route and protect the optional cloud request. This optional, ephemeral processing is used for app functionality and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. The app Worker does not persist the IP address or Cloudflare invocation envelope and does not send either to the cloud AI provider.

Request, replay, selection, debit-idempotency, and deletion-barrier state are serialized in the same strongly consistent per-principal Cloudflare Durable Object. RevenueCat remains authoritative for the live COACH balance. The Worker stages a validated selection before asking RevenueCat to debit exactly one credit with an opaque idempotency key, then completes the replay record. A lost post-debit response remains recoverable with the same request identifier and idempotency key instead of being presented as a zero-debit terminal failure. RevenueCat webhooks are authenticated, deduplicated, and acknowledged but never cache or grant a credit balance. The configured Cloudflare KV is restricted to deduplicating one-way webhook event references for up to 7 days.

The Worker sends the cloud AI provider only the categorical fact identifiers and the offered static candidate definitions. It does not send Google or RevenueCat identifiers, the account binding, request identifier, media, raw tracking events, or locally rendered Coach text to that provider. The provider runs a pinned cloud AI model to return one offered card identifier or abstain. Kinetic Atlas renders reviewed copy for that identifier on the device; the model does not write user-facing instructions.

The Production cloud AI account uses zero data retention. The provider does not use request or response content for model training unless PierDo opts in. Its prompt cache can keep the bounded categorical prompt and model key/value cache in volatile memory for several minutes while serving requests. That cache is not a persistent Coach record, but it is not treated as real-time ephemeral processing. The provider may retain limited operational metadata under its own service and security policies.

The local Atlas remains authoritative. The cloud Coach cannot change the exercise, rung, sets, reps, hold duration, rest, progression, safety content, or technique content selected by the app. It does not assess technique, infer recovery or fatigue, or make medical decisions.

Google Sign-In And Purchases

Google Sign-In is optional for the app as a whole and required for cloud Coach requests and Coach Pack purchase or restore. Google processes the sign-in interaction and returns an ID token and account identifier. Google may also return account email, name, profile, and token-time claims to the device. Those claims are optional and are processed ephemerally by Cloudflare only to authenticate the optional cloud Coach request. The Worker does not store the raw token or profile claims, and Kinetic Atlas does not send them to the cloud AI provider.

Kinetic Atlas uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat for one-time purchases, entitlement status, and Coach-credit balance. The non-consumable Kinetic Atlas Pro purchase permanently unlocks unlimited local camera-counted sessions; it does not include cloud Coach credits. A repeatable 365 Atlas Coach selections purchase grants 365 non-expiring COACH credits to the signed-in account. RevenueCat is configured with a passively restored verified Google subject when one is available; otherwise it may create a persistent anonymous App User ID for local lifetime Pro. Coach Pack purchase, restore, and cloud use require the current RevenueCat App User ID to match the verified Google subject. Signing out detaches the RevenueCat account on that device and clears its cached Coach-credit balance. Coach credits stay with the linked Google account. Lifetime Pro stays with its verified Google Play and RevenueCat purchase owner; the app retains a bounded one-way owner binding for a previously verified lifetime grant so Coach identity changes do not relock the independent local camera purchase. RevenueCat processes required, non-ephemeral purchase history, product identifiers, receipts or purchase tokens, entitlement and balance status, last-seen time, device type, and operating-system information for purchase functionality, account management, and purchase analytics.

The app does not store payment-card details. Payment details are handled by Google Play.

Security, Retention, And Deletion

Coach, purchase, and entitlement requests are encrypted in transit. The Worker does not log request bodies, authorization headers, raw identifiers, fact or candidate context, or provider responses. Redacted operational events contain only closed status values and a truncated one-way request reference; they are kept for up to 7 days, with a maximum of 2,000 events.

The Production Worker explicitly disables Cloudflare persisted Worker logs, invocation logs, and traces. After deployment, Cloudflare script settings returned a null observability object, which is Cloudflare's canonical disabled result. Logpush is disabled and no tail consumer is attached. A temporary live tail would require a deliberate, bounded operator action and could expose the transient IP address and coarse request location; policy forbids capturing, exporting, or persisting that envelope and requires the tail to be stopped immediately after the diagnostic.

The Worker temporarily retains:

  • generating and pre-debit staged records for up to 2 minutes;
  • unresolved debit-resolution, retiring, and compensating records without a time-based expiry until exact recovery or authenticated same-account reconciliation;
  • completed same-request replay records for up to 32 days, including a one-way request hash and selected response but not the raw fact-ID list;
  • a one-way RevenueCat webhook event reference in KV for up to 7 days; and
  • a conservative provider-spend reservation for up to 24 hours.

Open pseudonymous principal state is automatically purged after 35 days without Coach activity, except that unresolved debit-resolution, retiring, or compensating state remains until reconciliation. Authenticated deletion first fences and scrubs old selection state, resolves an original orphan debit, and applies an exactly-once one-credit compensation if that abandoned debit committed. It then replaces live state with a minimal closed deletion-epoch tombstone containing only a schema version, deletion epoch, closed status, and last-activity time. It contains no facts, candidates, request results, selections, or cached balance. The tombstone blocks in-flight work and webhooks from recreating deleted state and is retained for no more than 35 days without later explicit cloud Coach access.

Cloudflare encrypts the SQLite Durable Object state at rest and maintains point-in-time recovery history for up to 30 days. Subject to the unresolved-debit retry behavior described below, authenticated deletion removes the live principal state immediately, but an older encrypted recovery snapshot can still contain the prior record during that recovery window. A Production restore is forbidden unless the incident procedure preserves the current deletion fences and reapplies every affected deletion before traffic reopens. If affected deleted principals cannot be identified and refenced, the snapshot must not be restored.

The dedicated Production cloud AI credential is installed and armed behind the Worker's global kill switch. One controlled cloud AI selection was completed on August 9, 2026; the global kill was immediately restored after the request. The Production logging, recovery, training, and retention configuration is recorded above and must be reverified after relevant provider or deployment changes. Google, Google Play, RevenueCat, Cloudflare, and the cloud AI provider may retain service, security, billing, or legal records under their own policies.

Cloud Coach Data Deletion

Authenticated cloud Coach deletion is available both in the app and through the external deletion portal. Sign in to the portal with the same Google account used for Kinetic Atlas, then confirm deletion. Either path fences and scrubs prior selection state, resolves an original orphan debit, applies an exactly-once one-credit compensation if that abandoned debit committed, removes the pseudonymous request, replay, selection, and debit-idempotency state from live storage, and leaves only the minimal closed deletion-epoch tombstone described above for up to 35 days. The portal discards its Google credential after success. App launch and passively restored consent do not make a Worker access request or reopen the tombstone. Creating new server-side Coach state later requires an intentional in-app AI Coach, cloud-consent, or sign-in action with a valid signed Google ID token. Google or Android may reuse a still-current token issued before deletion, so sign-in may not ask for credentials again. The deletion epoch and the Worker's request-start fence still prevent in-flight work and webhooks from reopening deleted state. As described above, older encrypted SQLite Durable Object recovery history can retain the prior record for up to 30 days, and any restore must preserve and reapply the deletion fences before traffic reopens. If the debit or compensation outcome remains ambiguous, deletion reports a resolving result and must be retried; it never reports success while the balance outcome is uncertain.

After successful in-app deletion, the app also clears its pending cloud request, revokes cloud Coach consent, and signs this device out of Coach. Web-portal deletion cannot change data already stored on a phone or tablet. Locally saved Coach cards, Coach outcomes, and training history remain until you clear app storage or uninstall. A redacted deletion event can remain for up to 7 days, and a spend reservation can remain for up to 24 hours. Except for the automatic exactly-once compensation of an abandoned unresolved debit, cloud-state deletion does not refund completed selections or erase the authoritative RevenueCat COACH balance, the Google account, or Google Play and RevenueCat purchase records. Contact PierDo at pierdo.dev@gmail.com for privacy help or requests about purchase records; email is not required to use the authenticated deletion portal. Some records may need to be retained for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, security, or platform-support reasons.

Cloudflare and the cloud AI provider process limited operational metadata needed to operate and protect the optional cloud Coach.
